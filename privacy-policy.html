Privacy Policy

How Ben Ryan Real Estate Services collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 2026

Who We Are

Ben Ryan Real Estate Services is operated by Benjamin Ryan, a California licensed real estate broker (DRE #01005833), located at 1187 Coast Village Rd #110, Santa Barbara, CA 93108. This policy explains how we handle information collected through benryanrealestate.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact details (name, email address, phone number) submitted through our contact form
  • Property and transaction details submitted through our BPO (Broker Price Opinion) order form, including property address, loan or file number, and lender or AMC contact information
  • Any additional information you choose to include in a message to us

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit the site, such as pages viewed and general browser/device information, through the analytics tools described below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and fulfill BPO and real estate service requests
  • To communicate with you about your transaction, order, or question
  • To send occasional market updates or newsletters, only if you've opted in
  • To understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate this site:

  • Google Analytics — aggregate, anonymized site usage statistics
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymized session insights that help us understand how the site is used
  • MailerLite — email delivery for anyone who opts into updates
  • Cloudflare — hosting, security, and content delivery

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data they process on our behalf.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies, primarily to support the analytics tools listed above. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention & Security

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose it was collected for, including recordkeeping required by California real estate regulations. We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission for all data submitted through this site.

Your Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to request access to, or deletion of, the personal information we hold about them, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to ben@benryanrealestate.com or 805-565-8900.
