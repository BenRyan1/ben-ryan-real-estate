Who This Is For

Situations Ben sees often

Trust and estate sales

A family member has passed, or a trust needs to be settled, and a Santa Barbara property is part of it. Timing, paperwork, and decisions all arrive at once.

Reverse mortgage payoff

The loan has come due, or heirs are weighing whether to pay it off, refinance, or sell. The numbers are easier to see when someone walks through them with you.

Long-time owners

Decades of ownership, a lot of equity, and a real question: stay, downsize, or sell. You deserve straight answers before you decide anything.

How It Works

Three simple steps

  1. You reach out. Tell Ben a little about the property and your situation. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
  2. A plain-language conversation. Ben explains the options, the typical timeline, and what to ask your attorney or CPA. If a sale makes sense, he will say so. If it does not, he will say that too.
  3. Introduction to a licensed partner broker. If you decide to sell, Ben introduces you to a trusted licensed broker who handles the listing and sale.
Referral disclosure. Ben Ryan Real Estate Services may receive a referral fee from the partner brokerage if you choose to list with them. You are never required to use the broker Ben introduces, and you pay nothing to Ben for the introduction or the conversation.
Helpful To Gather

A short checklist

  • The property address and, if you have it, the latest property tax bill
  • Any current mortgage or reverse mortgage statement
  • Contact details for the family attorney, trustee, or CPA, if one is involved
  • Your rough timeline (no deadline is fine)
  • Your questions, written down as they come to mind
Important. This page is general information, not legal or tax advice. Estate, trust, and tax questions belong with your attorney and CPA. Ben works alongside them.
