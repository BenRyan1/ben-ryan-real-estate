About Ben Ryan

Your Broker With Exceptional Local Knowledge

Ben Ryan has worked in the Santa Barbara real estate market since 1988, thirty-eight years through multiple market cycles and changing economic conditions: the late-1980s regional downturn, the dot-com years, the 2008 credit crisis, the pandemic-era boom, and today's insurance-and-financing bind. His experience includes residential and commercial transactions, investment properties, buyer and seller representation, and specialized BPO and REO services.

That experience is valuable because every market is different, and every property and transaction has its own circumstances.

The goal isn’t simply to close a transaction. It’s to help you make a good real estate decision.

Broker Price Opinion Services

Ben Ryan provides USPAP-compliant Broker Price Opinions for banks, mortgage servicers, hedge funds, and asset management firms operating in the Santa Barbara market.

  • Fast turnaround — typically 48–72 hours
  • REO pre-listing and disposition valuations
  • Short sale and pre-foreclosure analysis
  • Occupancy and condition verification
  • Market trend and absorption rate analysis
