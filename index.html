About Ben Ryan

Decades of Santa Barbara Market Expertise

Benjamin Ryan is a California licensed real estate broker who has worked the Santa Barbara market since 1988, thirty-eight years across five distinct market cycles: the late-1980s regional downturn, the dot-com years, the 2008 credit crisis, the pandemic-era boom, and today's insurance-and-financing bind. His work spans residential and commercial transactions, investment properties, and specialized default services including BPO valuations and REO asset management.

Ben's depth of local market knowledge, from the bluffs of Montecito to the neighborhoods of Goleta, gives clients and institutional clients alike an edge in one of California's most competitive coastal markets.
